Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how Aviyo delivers for your business.

Deliveries
What areas do you deliver to?+
We currently cover active zones across the GTA, including Downtown Toronto, Vaughan, Mississauga, Brampton, North York, Etobicoke, and Scarborough. Bramalea and Willowdale are coming soon.
What are your delivery times?+
Evening pickup for next-day delivery, or morning pickup by 9:30am for same-day. There's no price difference between the two.
Do you offer tracking?+
Every delivery comes with photo proof of completion. Live GPS tracking isn't available yet, but it's on our roadmap.
What types of items can you deliver?+
Packages under 15 lbs and no larger than 18"×18"×18" — a good fit for most e-commerce grocery and dry goods orders.
What happens if a delivery can't be completed?+
We hold the package for 48 hours and attempt re-delivery. The re-delivery fee is waived for founding clients during their first 6 months.
How do I file a claim if something's lost or damaged?+
Claims can be filed within 5 business days of delivery. Liability is capped at $75 per package.
Pricing & Partnerships
How much does it cost?+
Pricing is tailored based on your delivery volume and needs. Please contact us and we'll walk you through the details.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract?+
No. You submit a delivery list whenever you have packages to send — there's no long-term contract required.
